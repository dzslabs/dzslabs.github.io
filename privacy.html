Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Pre-launch draft
01

Introduction

DZS Labs is a software brand of DZS Infinity LLC. This page is a pre-launch privacy notice for the DZS Labs website and products currently in development. Product-specific privacy terms will be finalized before any public release.

02

Information we collect

The public website may collect information that you choose to provide through a connected contact form or business email. The current static build does not send contact-form submissions because no verified form backend has been configured.

03

Information collected through DZS Labs products

SlideBatch and ShelfReady Sheets are currently in development and are not publicly available. Before release, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what each product accesses, processes, stores, and shares.

04

How information is used

When collection is enabled, information will be used only for the purposes disclosed at the point of collection, such as responding to inquiries, providing product functionality, maintaining security, and meeting legal obligations.

05

Google Workspace / Google API data

Some DZS Labs products are intended to integrate with Google Workspace. The final policy for each released product will identify the Google API scopes used, the data accessed, the purpose of that access, storage behavior, and deletion procedures. DZS Labs will not claim broader access than the product actually requires.

06

Third-party service providers

Before launch, DZS Labs will identify material third-party providers used for hosting, analytics, support, payments, or other operational functions where disclosure is required. This page intentionally does not invent providers that have not been selected.

07

Data retention

Product-specific retention periods have not yet been finalized. Before public release, DZS Labs will publish the retention and deletion rules that correspond to the actual behavior of each product and supporting service.

08

Data security

DZS Labs intends to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the data processed. Specific security claims or certifications will only be published when they can be supported.

09

User rights

Depending on applicable law and the data actually collected, users may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other handling of personal information. Final procedures will be published before launch.

10

Changes to this policy

This pre-launch notice will change as the products and supporting infrastructure are finalized. The page will show an updated date whenever material privacy information changes.

11

Contact

A verified privacy or business contact address will be added before public launch. No email address is invented on this draft site.

12

Legal entity information

DZS Labs is a software brand owned and operated by DZS Infinity LLC. DZS Infinity LLC is the legal entity responsible for DZS Labs products and business operations.
