Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DZS Labs, a software brand owned and operated by DZS Infinity LLC, collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our website and products and services.

1. Introduction

DZS Labs (operated by DZS Infinity LLC) values your privacy. This Policy describes the types of information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Our website and products are designed for productivity and small-business workflows.

As our products evolve, additional details about data practices for specific features may be provided at launch. If we make material changes to this Policy, we will update the “Last updated” date and post the revised version on this page.

2. Information We Collect

The current public website is primarily informational. The contact form included on this site is not configured to transmit submissions unless a form-delivery endpoint is added. If that changes, this Policy should be updated to describe the information transmitted and the service used to process it.

3. How Information Is Used

Any information intentionally submitted through a future configured contact channel would be used to respond to the inquiry and handle related product, business, marketplace, privacy, or legal matters. No broader use is stated here because product-specific data practices are not yet finalized.

4. Google Workspace Data

The current public website does not request access to Google Workspace account data. Product-specific Google Workspace or Google API access, scopes, storage, and processing practices will be documented before any applicable product is made publicly available.

5. Data Retention

No product-specific retention period is stated at this pre-launch stage. When a product or contact-delivery service is made public, any applicable retention practices will be documented here or in the relevant product documentation.

6. Data Security

DZS Labs does not claim a certification, encryption standard, or security control that has not been specifically established. Product-specific security information will be described when those systems are available.

7. Your Rights

Privacy rights can depend on where you live and on the type of information involved. Once a public data-processing feature is active, requests concerning applicable personal information can be made through the contact channel published on this website.

8. Children's Privacy

DZS Labs products are intended for productivity and business workflows, not for use as services directed to children. This section will be updated if a future product requires more specific treatment.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be updated as the website and products evolve. Material revisions will be reflected by changing the date shown at the top of this page and publishing the updated policy here.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be submitted through the DZS Labs contact page. DZS Labs is a software brand owned and operated by DZS Infinity LLC.
