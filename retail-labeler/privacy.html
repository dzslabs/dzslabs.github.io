RETAIL LABELER Product Privacy Notice

Effective Date: November 5, 2026
Last Updated: November 5, 2026

This Product Privacy Notice explains how RETAIL LABELER, a Google Sheets Editor add-on operated by DZS Infinity LLC under the DZS Labs brand, accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the add-on.

This notice supplements the general DZS Labs Privacy Policy available at:

https://dzslabs.site/privacy.html

If this Product Privacy Notice provides more specific information about RETAIL LABELER than the general DZS Labs Privacy Policy, this Product Privacy Notice applies to RETAIL LABELER.

1. About RETAIL LABELER

RETAIL LABELER is a Google Sheets Editor add-on that helps users create and print product, price, and barcode labels using information stored in their Google Sheets spreadsheets.

The add-on allows users to select relevant spreadsheet data, configure label layouts, and generate print-ready PDF output.

Depending on the features used, RETAIL LABELER may provide functionality including:

  • selecting spreadsheet columns for label content;
  • mapping spreadsheet fields to label elements;
  • creating product and price labels;
  • generating barcode labels from available product identifiers;
  • configuring label layouts and dimensions;
  • adding a logo to supported label layouts;
  • previewing label output; and
  • generating PDF files for printing.

RETAIL LABELER is designed to process the spreadsheet information required for the label-generation workflow.

The MVP does not provide supplier delivery processing, supplier receiving workflows, supplier-file import and reconciliation, or full inventory management.

2. Google Permissions Used by RETAIL LABELER

RETAIL LABELER requests only the Google permissions required to provide its disclosed functionality.

The application uses the following OAuth scopes.

Google Sheets — Current Spreadsheet Only

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly

RETAIL LABELER uses this permission to access the Google Sheets spreadsheet in which the user is currently working.

This access is required to read the spreadsheet data selected for label generation and to provide the add-on's spreadsheet-based functionality.

The permission is limited to the current spreadsheet context.

RETAIL LABELER does not request the broad Google Sheets scope to access all spreadsheets in the user's Google Account.

Google Drive — Application Data

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

RETAIL LABELER uses this permission to store and retrieve application-specific data in Google Drive's application data storage area where required by the add-on.

This may include product configuration, saved settings, and template-related information needed to provide the add-on's functionality.

This permission does not provide general access to the user's Google Drive files.

RETAIL LABELER does not request the broad Google Drive scope or the drive.readonly scope.

External Requests

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.external_request

RETAIL LABELER uses this permission to make external network requests required for supported product services, including license activation and validation and limited product analytics.

This permission does not itself provide access to additional Google Account data.

Any Google user data obtained through Google APIs remains subject to the uses and restrictions described in this notice.

Google Sheets Container Interface

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui

RETAIL LABELER uses this permission to display and operate its user interface within Google Sheets.

This permission supports the add-on interface and its interaction with the spreadsheet environment. It does not provide general access to unrelated Google Account data.

3. Google User Data We Access

RETAIL LABELER processes Google user data only as necessary to provide the label-generation functionality requested by the user.

Google Sheets Data

The add-on may read information from the spreadsheet in which the user is working.

Depending on the columns selected or mapped by the user, this information may include:

  • product names;
  • product descriptions;
  • SKUs and other product identifiers;
  • barcode values;
  • prices; and
  • other spreadsheet fields selected by the user for label content.

This information is used to create and format the labels requested by the user.

RETAIL LABELER does not need access to unrelated spreadsheets or unrelated Google Account content to provide its core functionality.

Spreadsheet Configuration and Mapping Information

RETAIL LABELER may process configuration information associated with the current spreadsheet, such as selected columns, field mappings, and label-generation settings.

This information is used to reproduce the user's chosen label configuration and support the add-on's workflow.

Google Drive Application Data

Where the add-on saves application-specific settings or template-related information, it may store that information in Google Drive application data storage.

This storage is used to support RETAIL LABELER functionality and is not a general-purpose product database.

4. Label Content and PDF Generation

RETAIL LABELER uses the spreadsheet information selected by the user to generate label content.

The label-generation workflow may process product names, identifiers, prices, barcode values, formatting settings, and other selected fields to produce the requested output.

PDF generation is performed client-side in the user's browser.

The resulting PDF is provided for the user's printing workflow. RETAIL LABELER does not require the user to upload the complete product catalog to a separate DZS Labs product database to generate labels.

Users remain responsible for reviewing the generated labels and confirming that the content is correct before printing.

5. How We Use Your Data

RETAIL LABELER uses information only as necessary to provide and maintain its user-facing functionality.

This includes using selected spreadsheet data and configuration information to:

  • read the spreadsheet fields selected by the user;
  • map spreadsheet columns to label content;
  • create product, price, and barcode labels;
  • apply the selected label layout and dimensions;
  • render supported barcode content;
  • apply supported logo and formatting settings;
  • display label previews; and
  • generate print-ready PDF output.

RETAIL LABELER also uses the limited product analytics described in Section 13 to measure product usage and reliability.

License information is used for the activation and validation of paid product features, as described in Section 14.

Google user data is not used for unrelated purposes.

6. Raw, Aggregated, Anonymized, and Derived Google User Data

RETAIL LABELER accesses the raw Google user data described in this notice only as necessary to provide the user-facing functionality requested by the user.

The product may generate limited derived operational information necessary to map spreadsheet fields, configure label output, render barcode content, and generate the requested PDF.

RETAIL LABELER does not request separate aggregated or anonymized datasets from Google APIs.

Raw Google user data, and any information aggregated, anonymized, de-identified, or derived from that data, remains subject to the same use, transfer, security, retention, and Limited Use restrictions described in this notice.

RETAIL LABELER does not use raw, aggregated, anonymized, de-identified, or derived Google user data for advertising, profiling, data brokerage, creditworthiness, lending, generalized AI/ML model training, or unrelated purposes.

7. Data Storage and Retention

RETAIL LABELER retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice.

Product configuration and template-related information may be stored using Google Workspace storage mechanisms, including Google Drive application data storage and spreadsheet-associated storage, as applicable to the feature.

The product does not operate a separate DZS Labs product database or centralized product catalog for storing users' spreadsheet product information.

PDF output is generated client-side in the user's browser. The user controls the resulting file and any subsequent storage or printing of that file.

Analytics information processed through Google Analytics is subject to the retention settings configured for the DZS Labs Google Analytics property and Google's applicable service terms.

License-related information may be retained as reasonably necessary to activate and validate licenses, prevent misuse, and maintain relevant business records.

When stored information is no longer reasonably necessary for its disclosed product purpose, it is deleted, anonymized, or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable law, Google platform requirements, and the DZS Labs Privacy Policy.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

DZS Labs does not sell Google user data.

RETAIL LABELER does not transfer spreadsheet product content, including product names, SKUs, barcode values, or prices, to non-Google third-party service providers as part of its core label-generation workflow.

The restrictions in this section apply to raw Google user data and to information aggregated, anonymized, de-identified, or derived from Google user data.

Google user data processed by RETAIL LABELER is not shared or transferred to third parties for:

  • targeted advertising;
  • personalized advertising;
  • retargeting;
  • interest-based advertising;
  • advertising profiling;
  • sale to data brokers;
  • sale to information resellers;
  • determining creditworthiness;
  • lending purposes; or
  • developing, improving, or training artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Limited pseudonymous product analytics and license-validation information may be transmitted to the specific service providers described in Sections 13 and 14. These transmissions are limited to the information needed for those separate services and are not intended to include spreadsheet product content.

Information may also be disclosed where permitted by applicable law and Google policies, such as where necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or valid legal process.

9. Google API Services User Data Policy

RETAIL LABELER's use and transfer of raw or derived user data received from Google Workspace APIs adheres to the applicable Google User Data and Developer Policies, including the Limited Use requirements.

These restrictions apply to raw Google user data and to information aggregated, anonymized, de-identified, or derived from Google user data.

Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing functionality that is visible and relevant to the user.

RETAIL LABELER does not use Google user data for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, data brokerage, profiling, or other prohibited purposes.

RETAIL LABELER does not use raw, aggregated, anonymized, de-identified, or derived data received from Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve, or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

RETAIL LABELER does not transfer Google Workspace API user data to third-party artificial intelligence or machine-learning services.

Human access to Google user data is limited to circumstances permitted by applicable Google policies, such as when:

  • the user has provided appropriate authorization;
  • access is necessary for security purposes;
  • access is required by law; or
  • another use is expressly permitted by Google's applicable policies.

10. Security

DZS Labs uses reasonable administrative, organizational, and technical measures designed to protect information processed by RETAIL LABELER against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, destruction, or loss.

Access to Google user data is limited to the permissions and functionality required by the product.

RETAIL LABELER is designed to minimize the transfer of spreadsheet product information outside the user's Google Workspace environment.

No method of electronic processing or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Advertising and Data Monetization

RETAIL LABELER does not use Google user data for advertising.

Google user data is not sold.

Google user data is not used for personalized advertising, retargeting, interest-based advertising, advertising profiles, or data brokerage.

Any monetization of RETAIL LABELER through paid product features or licenses does not change these restrictions on the use of Google user data.

12. Third-Party Services

RETAIL LABELER uses certain third-party services for functions separate from its core spreadsheet label-generation workflow.

These services may include:

  • Google Workspace and Google Apps Script — for the add-on environment, spreadsheet access, application interface, and Google Workspace storage.
  • Google Analytics 4 — for limited pseudonymous product usage analytics.
  • Lemon Squeezy — for payment processing and license activation or validation.
  • External asset delivery services — where required to load application resources.

These providers process information according to their own applicable privacy policies and service terms.

The product-specific data transmitted to analytics and licensing services is described in the following sections.

13. Product Analytics and Diagnostics

RETAIL LABELER uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect limited product usage analytics.

DZS Labs uses this analytics information to understand usage of the RETAIL LABELER workflow, measure product reliability, identify general usage patterns, and improve the product.

The analytics implementation is intended to collect limited pseudonymous product events and technical usage information.

Analytics information may include:

  • a pseudonymous analytics identifier;
  • the RETAIL LABELER product identifier;
  • product lifecycle or feature-usage events;
  • general label-generation activity;
  • selected feature or layout usage; and
  • limited technical or diagnostic information associated with product operation.

RETAIL LABELER does not intend to send the following spreadsheet content to Google Analytics:

  • product names;
  • product descriptions;
  • SKUs;
  • barcode values;
  • retail prices;
  • spreadsheet cell contents;
  • spreadsheet filenames;
  • full product catalog data; or
  • generated label content.

The limited analytics information described above is transmitted to Google Analytics, a service provided by Google LLC.

DZS Labs uses this information for product usage measurement, reliability measurement, diagnostics, and product improvement.

It is not used for advertising, personalized advertising, profiling, data brokerage, creditworthiness, lending, or developing, improving, or training artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Analytics processing is separate from the core label-generation operation. A failure of the analytics service does not prevent RETAIL LABELER from performing its user-requested label-generation functionality.

14. License Activation and Payment Processing

RETAIL LABELER may use Lemon Squeezy to process purchases and manage paid product licenses.

Lemon Squeezy acts as the payment and licensing service provider for applicable transactions.

When a user purchases a paid license, Lemon Squeezy may independently collect payment, billing, transaction, and contact information under its own privacy policy and terms.

DZS Labs does not receive or store full payment card details.

For license activation and validation, RETAIL LABELER may communicate with the Lemon Squeezy License API.

This process may involve:

  • the license key provided by the user;
  • a product or license identifier;
  • an instance identifier associated with the installation or workbook; and
  • license activation or validation status.

This information is used to activate and validate access to paid RETAIL LABELER features, manage license status, and prevent unauthorized use.

Spreadsheet product content, including product names, SKUs, barcode values, and prices, is not required for license validation.

License processing is separate from the core label-generation workflow.

15. Removing Access

Users can remove RETAIL LABELER's access to their Google Account through Google's account permission controls.

Removing authorization prevents the application from continuing to use that authorization to access Google services.

Users may also uninstall RETAIL LABELER through the applicable Google Workspace interface.

Removing authorization or uninstalling the add-on does not necessarily delete information already stored independently of the Google authorization, such as applicable license or analytics records.

Users may contact DZS Labs regarding applicable stored product information or deletion requests.

16. Data Deletion Requests

RETAIL LABELER does not require users to create a separate DZS Labs account.

Users can revoke RETAIL LABELER's access to their Google Account through Google's account permission settings.

Information stored in the user's spreadsheet or Google Drive remains subject to the user's Google Workspace controls.

If RETAIL LABELER retains applicable personal information independently of the user's Google Workspace content, users may request deletion by contacting:

PRIVACY@DZSLABS.SITE

Deletion requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law, Google platform requirements, and the DZS Labs Privacy Policy.

Certain information may be retained where reasonably necessary or legally required for license administration, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, or legal compliance.

17. Children

RETAIL LABELER is a business productivity tool and is not designed to independently collect personal information from children.

The product processes spreadsheet content selected by the user to provide the requested label-generation functionality.

Where the add-on is used in an educational or organizational environment, the user or organization using the product is responsible for ensuring that its use complies with applicable institutional policies, consent requirements, and law.
