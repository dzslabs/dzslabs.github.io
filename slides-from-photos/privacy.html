Slides from Photos

Product Privacy Notice

Effective Date: October 5, 2026
Last Updated: October 5, 2026

This Product Privacy Notice explains how Slides from Photos, a Google Slides add-on operated by DZS Infinity LLC under the DZS Labs brand, accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the add-on.

This notice supplements the general DZS Labs Privacy Policy available at:

https://dzslabs.site/privacy.html

If this Product Privacy Notice provides more specific information about Slides from Photos than the general DZS Labs Privacy Policy, this Product Privacy Notice applies to Slides from Photos.

1. About Slides from Photos

Slides from Photos is a Google Slides Editor add-on that helps users turn selected photos into Google Slides, creating one photo per slide.

Users can select photos from:

  • Google Drive;
  • Google Photos; or
  • their computer.

The add-on also provides features including:

  • configurable photo ordering;
  • Fit and Fill image placement;
  • batch progress tracking;
  • Resume for supported interrupted jobs;
  • photo-set verification; and
  • completed, skipped, and failed processing status.

2. Google Permissions Used by Slides from Photos

Slides from Photos requests only the Google permissions required to provide its disclosed functionality.

The production application uses the following OAuth scopes:

Google Slides

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/presentations

Slides from Photos uses this permission to create and update slides in the Google Slides presentation being processed by the user.

The add-on uses this access only to provide the photo-to-slides functionality requested by the user.

Google Drive

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

Slides from Photos uses this permission when the user selects photos from Google Drive.

This permission is limited to files that the user selects, creates, or otherwise makes available to the application through Google's supported file-selection and authorization mechanisms.

Slides from Photos does not request the broad Google Drive scope or the drive.readonly scope.

Google Photos Picker

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/photospicker.mediaitems.readonly

Slides from Photos uses this permission when the user explicitly chooses Google Photos as the photo source.

The application processes media items selected by the user through the Google Photos Picker for the purpose of creating the requested slides.

Slides from Photos does not use this permission to browse the user's entire Google Photos library independently of the user's selection.

External requests

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.external_request

This permission allows the add-on to make network requests required for supported product functionality.

It does not itself provide access to additional Google Account data.

Any Google user data obtained through Google APIs remains subject to the uses and restrictions described in this notice.

Google Slides container interface

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui

Slides from Photos uses this permission to display and operate its user interface within Google Slides.

This permission is used to provide the add-on interface and does not provide general access to unrelated Google Account data.

3. Google User Data We Access

Depending on the source and features selected by the user, Slides from Photos may process the following Google user data.

Google Slides data

The add-on may access information required to create or update slides in the presentation in which the user is working.

This access is used to:

  • create slides;
  • place selected photos onto slides;
  • apply the selected Fit or Fill behavior;
  • continue supported batch operations; and
  • report processing results.

Google Drive data

When Google Drive is selected as the source, Slides from Photos processes only the Drive files the user chooses or otherwise makes available to the application.

This may include information necessary to process the selected files, such as file content and associated file metadata required for the requested operation.

Google Photos data

When Google Photos is selected as the source, Slides from Photos processes only media items explicitly selected through the Google Photos Picker.

The selected media is used to create the slides requested by the user and to support the associated batch workflow.

4. Photos Selected From Your Computer

Slides from Photos also allows users to select image files from their computer.

These files are not accessed through Google Drive or Google Photos permissions.

Local files are processed only after the user explicitly selects them for use with Slides from Photos.

They are used for the requested photo-to-slides operation and related batch functionality.

5. How We Use Your Data

Slides from Photos uses information only as necessary to provide and maintain its user-facing functionality.

This includes using selected photos and related information to:

  • create one slide per selected photo;
  • determine the selected photo order;
  • apply Fit or Fill placement;
  • track batch progress;
  • continue supported interrupted batches;
  • verify that a resumed batch corresponds to the originally selected photos;
  • identify processing problems;
  • calculate completed, skipped, and failed items; and
  • display the final processing result.

Google user data is not used for unrelated purposes.

6. Batch Progress and Resume Data

Slides from Photos maintains limited processing state and job metadata necessary to support features such as:

  • batch progress;
  • Resume;
  • photo-set verification;
  • completed, skipped, and failed accounting; and
  • recovery from supported interruptions.

This job information is used only for the operation and reliability of Slides from Photos.

It is not used for advertising or profiling.

The job state is separate from the user's Google authorization itself and exists only to support the requested Slides from Photos workflow.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Slides from Photos retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, including supported batch processing and Resume functionality.

Processing state associated with a batch may be retained when necessary to allow the user to continue an interrupted job.

Data is not retained merely for advertising, data brokerage, or unrelated profiling purposes.

When stored information is no longer required for its disclosed product purpose, it is handled according to the applicable product lifecycle and the DZS Labs Privacy Policy.

Users may contact DZS Labs regarding privacy or deletion requests using the contact information provided below.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

DZS Labs does not sell Google user data.

Google user data processed by Slides from Photos is not shared or transferred to third parties for:

  • targeted advertising;
  • personalized advertising;
  • retargeting;
  • interest-based advertising;
  • sale to data brokers;
  • sale to information resellers;
  • determining creditworthiness;
  • lending purposes; or
  • training generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Information may be disclosed only where permitted by the Google API Services User Data Policy and applicable law, such as:

  • when necessary to provide the user-facing functionality requested by the user;
  • to service providers acting on behalf of DZS Labs where necessary to operate the product and where permitted by applicable Google policies;
  • for security or abuse prevention;
  • where required by applicable law or valid legal process; or
  • with the user's explicit authorization where required.

Any permitted service provider access is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

9. Google API Services User Data Policy

Slides from Photos' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing functionality that is visible and relevant to the user.

Slides from Photos does not use Google user data for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, data brokerage, or other prohibited purposes.

Slides from Photos does not use Google Workspace API data to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Human access to Google user data is limited to circumstances permitted by applicable Google policies, such as when:

  • the user has provided appropriate authorization;
  • access is necessary for security purposes;
  • access is required by law; or
  • another use is expressly permitted by Google's applicable policies.

Google's current OAuth verification guidance specifically requires disclosures about data access, use, sharing, security, retention/deletion, and prohibited uses such as advertising or generalized AI/ML training. Google Help

10. Security

DZS Labs uses reasonable administrative, organizational, and technical measures designed to protect information processed by Slides from Photos against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, destruction, or loss.

Access to Google user data is limited to the permissions and functionality required by the product.

No method of electronic processing or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Advertising and Data Monetization

Slides from Photos does not use Google user data for advertising.

Google user data is not sold.

Google user data is not used for personalized advertising, retargeting, interest-based advertising, advertising profiles, or data brokerage.

Any future monetization of Slides from Photos, including paid product features or subscriptions, does not change these restrictions on the use of Google user data.

12. Analytics and Diagnostics

Slides from Photos may process limited technical or diagnostic information where necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, or improve the reliability of the product.

Any analytics or diagnostic information used by the product must be limited to the disclosed operational purpose and must not override the restrictions applicable to Google user data.

Google user data obtained through OAuth permissions is not used to create advertising profiles.

13. Removing Access

Users can remove Slides from Photos' access to their Google Account through Google's account permission controls.

Removing authorization prevents the application from continuing to use that authorization to access Google services.

Uninstalling or removing authorization does not necessarily remove processing state that may already exist independently of the Google authorization where retention is still necessary or permitted.

Users may contact DZS Labs to request information about applicable stored product data or deletion.

Slides from Photos does not require users to create a separate DZS Labs account.

Users can revoke Slides from Photos' access to their Google Account at any time through their Google Account permission settings.
